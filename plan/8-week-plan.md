# 8-Week Home Dumbbell Strength + Hypertrophy Plan

## Plan summary

- Goal: increase strength and muscle mass
- Session style: little and often
- Session length: 30 to 45 minutes (up to 50 minutes on full-coverage days)
- Equipment: 8 kg, 10 kg, 12.5 kg, 19.5 kg dumbbells; 15 lbs and 12 lbs pairs; bench, mat; INNSTAR Advanced full-body pack (steel bar, handles, door anchors, bands)
- Rotation: Push, Lower A, Pull, Lower B
- Frequency: 3 to 5 strength sessions per week, adjusted around running and padel
- Total training exposures: usually 5 to 6 per week when lifting and sport are combined
- Week 4: deload
- Week 8: rep-PR week

## Hybrid week guidance (running + padel + lifting)

- Running and padel count as real lower-body workload.
- Keep both lower days in the rotation, but use a light lower day when legs are not recovered.
- Light lower day = first 2 lower lifts only, 1 fewer set each, and stop with 2 to 3 reps in reserve.
- Progress lower-body targets only from normal lower sessions, not from light/fatigued sessions.

## Weekly structure

### Weeks 1 to 3

- Build reps inside the target ranges.
- Keep most sets at about 1 to 3 reps in reserve.
- Do not grind except maybe the last set of the first main lift.

### Week 4 deload

- Do 2 work sets per exercise instead of 3.
- Stop each set with about 3 to 4 reps in reserve.
- Use the same loads or a slightly easier variation.
- Keep the movement patterns the same.

### Weeks 5 to 7

- Continue rep progression.
- If an exercise is load-limited, add tempo or pauses before adding extra volume.
- Keep effort controlled and repeatable.

### Week 8 performance

- On the first main lift of the day, aim to beat your best set or total reps from the cycle.
- Keep 1 to 2 back-off sets after the top effort.
- Keep accessories clean and moderate.

## Session templates

### Push

1. Dumbbell bench press
   - 3 sets
   - Target: 12 to 15 reps
   - Start: 2 x 19.5 kg
   - Rest: 90 seconds
   - Default progression: add reps first; once 3 x 15 is solid, use 3-second eccentric or 1-second pause on chest
2. One-arm overhead press
   - 3 sets per arm
   - Target: 8 to 12 reps
   - Start: 12.5 kg
   - Rest: 60 to 75 seconds
   - Progression: reps, then slower lowering, then seated dead-stop starts if needed
3. Incline dumbbell press
   - 2 sets
   - Target: 10 to 15 reps
   - Start: 2 x 12.5 kg
   - Rest: 60 to 75 seconds
4. Lateral raise
   - 2 sets
   - Target: 12 to 20 reps
   - Start: 8 kg if form is strict, otherwise reduce range and slow down
   - Rest: 45 to 60 seconds
5. Overhead triceps extension
   - 2 sets
   - Target: 10 to 15 reps
   - Start: 10 kg or 12.5 kg as a single dumbbell
   - Rest: 45 to 60 seconds

### Lower A

1. Bulgarian split squat
   - 3 sets per leg
   - Target: 8 to 12 reps
   - Start: 2 x 12.5 kg
   - Rest: 75 to 90 seconds
   - Progression: reps, then longer bottom pause, then front-foot elevation
2. Romanian deadlift
   - 3 sets
   - Target: 10 to 15 reps
   - Start: 2 x 19.5 kg
   - Rest: 90 seconds
   - Progression: reps, then 3-second eccentric, then 1.5 reps if needed
3. Goblet squat
   - 2 sets
   - Target: 12 to 20 reps
   - Start: 19.5 kg
   - Rest: 60 to 75 seconds
   - Progression: reps, then 3-second eccentric, then 2-second pause in the hole
4. Calf raise
   - 2 sets per side
   - Target: 12 to 20 reps
   - Start: bodyweight or one 19.5 kg dumbbell
   - Rest: 30 to 45 seconds
5. Plank
   - 2 sets
   - Target: 30 to 60 seconds
6. Side plank
   - 2 sets per side
   - Target: 20 to 40 seconds per side
   - Rest: 30 seconds

### Pull

1. One-arm dumbbell row
   - 3 sets per arm
   - Target: 10 to 15 reps
   - Start: 19.5 kg
   - Rest: 60 to 75 seconds
   - Progression: reps, then 2-second squeeze at top, then longer stretch at bottom
2. Door-anchor lat pulldown (INNSTAR)
   - 2 sets
   - Target: 10 to 15 reps
   - Start: moderate tension (clean reps with 1 to 2 reps in reserve)
   - Rest: 60 seconds
   - Progression: add reps first, then 1-second pause at chest, then band tension
3. Rear delt raise
   - 2 sets
   - Target: 12 to 20 reps
   - Start: 8 kg if strict, otherwise reduce momentum and use slower tempo
   - Rest: 45 to 60 seconds
4. Dumbbell pullover
   - 2 sets
   - Target: 10 to 15 reps
   - Start: 19.5 kg as a single dumbbell
   - Rest: 60 seconds
5. Hammer curl
   - 2 sets
   - Target: 8 to 15 reps
   - Start: 10 kg
   - Rest: 45 to 60 seconds
6. Dumbbell preacher curl
   - 2 sets
   - Target: 10 to 15 reps
   - Start: 8 kg
   - Rest: 45 to 60 seconds
   - Setup: brace upper arm on the back of an incline bench or against your thigh to minimize swing
7. Incline prone Y raise
   - 2 sets
   - Target: 10 to 15 reps
   - Start: bodyweight, very light range, or 12 lbs pair only if strict and pain-free
   - Rest: 45 to 60 seconds
   - Focus: lower traps and shoulder control

### Lower B

1. Reverse lunge
   - 3 sets per leg
   - Target: 8 to 12 reps
   - Start: 2 x 10 kg or 2 x 12.5 kg
   - Rest: 75 to 90 seconds
   - Progression: reps, then controlled descent, then deficit or longer stride if needed
2. Hip thrust or glute bridge
   - 3 sets
   - Target: 12 to 20 reps
   - Start: 19.5 kg across hips
   - Rest: 60 to 75 seconds
   - Progression: reps, then 2-second squeeze at top, then single-leg version
3. Single-leg Romanian deadlift
   - 2 sets per leg
   - Target: 8 to 12 reps
   - Start: 12.5 kg
   - Rest: 60 seconds
4. Paused goblet squat
   - 2 sets
   - Target: 10 to 15 reps
   - Start: 19.5 kg with 2-second pause
   - Rest: 60 to 75 seconds
5. Abs
   - 2 sets
   - Choose one: dead bug, hollow hold, or reverse crunch
   - Target: 10 to 20 reps or 20 to 40 seconds
6. Sliding leg curl (towel on smooth floor)
   - 2 sets
   - Target: 8 to 12 reps
   - Rest: 60 seconds
   - Focus: hamstring knee-flexion strength
7. Tibialis raise (wall supported)
   - 2 sets
   - Target: 15 to 25 reps
   - Rest: 30 seconds
   - Focus: shin and ankle resilience for running and padel

## Simple progression logic

### Main lifts

- Main lifts are bench press, Bulgarian split squat, Romanian deadlift, one-arm row, and reverse lunge.
- Keep the same load or variation until you reach the top of the rep range on all prescribed work sets with solid form.
- When you hit the top of the range, progress in this order:
  1. add tempo
  2. add pause
  3. increase range of motion
  4. use a harder unilateral version
  5. reduce rest by 10 to 15 seconds

### Accessories

- Add reps until you hit the top of the range.
- If the load is too easy, slow the eccentric and keep continuous tension.
- Do not chase failure on every accessory set.

## Practical effort guidance

- Most sessions should feel productive, not destructive.
- Leave a little in reserve on most sets.
- If performance drops hard for two sessions in a row, keep targets flat next time instead of forcing progression.
